Overview Foreign Body Airway Obstruction

Management of foreign-body airway obstruction has been integrated with basic life support as cardiopulmonary resuscitation is begun if a person with a foreign-body airway obstruction becomes unresponsive.

As noted in the Basic Life Support Overview section the initial knowledge conveyed by the American Heart Association is in a series of algorithms. The main purpose of this website is to help you memorize these algorithms so that you can use them in an emergency situation by the use of memory systems. Of course there is much more to being able to effectively provide basic life support than just memorizing the algorithms. The section on Preparedness discusses this further.

At the Algorithms: 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation and Emergency Cardiovascular Care website the are the following foreign body airway obstruction algoithms are listed under basic life support:

  • Infant Foreign Body Airway Obstruction

  • Child Foreign Body Airway Obstruction

  • Adult Foreign-Body Airway Obstruction Algorithm

The age ranges for the foreign body airway obstruction algorithms are defined as follows:
  • Infant: Less than one year old
  • Child: One year of age to signs of puberty (breast development in females and presence axiallary hair in males)
  • Adult: Signs of puberty and beyond (breast development in females and presence axiallary hair in males)
