Speeches

Memory methods have been used since ancient times for speeches by orators. First if one is to give a speech they should be knowledgeablew about which they are speeking. The problem is having prompts about the individual paragraphs in the speech to enable presentation in an orderly manner without forgetting importnat paragraphs.

I can testify when in medical training being able to give a rather lengthly presentation on a subject without refering to notes was a source of pride to me. At first you maybe be nervous that memory systems may not work under pressure (but they wiil) so take your notes with you but be susprised it you need to refer to them!

Intangible

There are many examples of intangible information in daily life that require substitute mental images such as personal names. If you meet a new acquintance named Jack who has a large nose as a distinguishing feature you might create a mental visual image of a car jack protruding from a nostril in his large nose to help you remember his name.

Galatians 5:22-23

After a Bible study I wished to remember the 9 fruits of the Spirit (love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control) for a daily personel prayer which was easily done in less than a minute using visual mnemonics and links.

I first pictured a glacier (Galatians) and since this is my only subject from Galatians I memorized I did not have to memorize chapter and verse. On the glacier is a red heart (love) lying flat and a person I know named Joy (Joy) standing on the heart. In her hands are a dove (peace) and hanging from the dove's mouth is a rescue basket stretcher with a patient (patience) in it. The patient is holding hold to one side a long piece of firewood kindling (kindness) and balanced on the far end of the kindling is a pan of Bananas Foster (goodness). In the pan of Bananas Foster are two praying hands (faithfulness). Held in the praying hands are a lamb (gentleness) and riding the lamb is a cowboy (self-control).

Of course I had to imbedded it stronger in my memory by repeating it several times the first day and now I just repeat it daily when I use it first thing in the morning.
